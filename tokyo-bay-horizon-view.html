A Panoramic Escape at the Edge of the Bay

The Tokyo Bay Horizon View is Sheraton's crown jewel — a breathtaking oceanfront retreat where the serene waters of Tokyo Bay unfold before you like a living canvas. Floor-to-ceiling windows frame panoramic vistas from sunrise to starlight, while Japanese-inspired interiors blend centuries-old aesthetics with modern luxury.

Wake to the gentle shimmer of dawn on the water, step onto your private balcony to feel the ocean breeze, and return to a sanctuary of warm wood tones, artisan furnishings, and spa-inspired indulgence. Every moment here is a conversation between land, sea, and sky.
