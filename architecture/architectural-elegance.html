Architectural Synergy with Nature

Design Paradigm

Embracing Tropical Modernism

Our resort architecture is grounded in Tropical Modernism — a design movement that celebrates warm climates, open pavilion structures, native materials, and lush biophilic integration.

  • Biophilic Light & Air Corridors: Naturally ventilated open-air colonnades capture ocean breeze and reduce air-conditioning reliance by 35%.
  • Native Teak & Louver Geometry: Sustainably sourced teak structures provide warm organic tactile contrast against clean architectural concrete.
  • Reflective Water Courtyards: Cascading water features lower ambient microclimate temperatures while creating soothing acoustic therapy.

Eco-Conscious Craftsmanship

Sustainable Materials

Every texture, stone, and beam at Sheraton is chosen with respect for nature, utilizing responsibly harvested, durable, and low-carbon materials.

FSC Certified

Reclaimed Sustainable Teak

Harvested from certified plantations and reclaimed architectural salvage. Natural teak oils resist marine climate weathering while aging into a distinguished silver-gray patina.

Local Sourced

Hand-Carved Volcanic Basalt

Quarried locally to minimize freight emissions. Dense thermal mass basalt walls absorb daytime tropical heat, regulating indoor temperatures naturally at night.

Energy Star

Low-E Insulated Smart Glass

High-performance double-glazed Low-E panels reflect 78% of solar heat gain while preserving pristine color-accurate vistas of the ocean horizon.
