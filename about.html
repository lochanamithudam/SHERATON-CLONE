About Sheraton

Welcome to Sheraton Colombo

The World's Gathering Place

Sheraton Colombo Hotel is more than just a place to stay—it is the world's gathering place. Situated in the heart of Sri Lanka's vibrant commercial capital along Galle Road, we offer a seamless blend of contemporary luxury, local Sri Lankan warmth, and intuitive service. Whether you are visiting for global business or coastal leisure, our commitment to excellence ensures an unforgettable stay.
