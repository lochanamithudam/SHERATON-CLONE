Sheraton Maui Resort & Spa

Nestled on the legendary Black Rock of Ka'anapali Beach, the Sheraton Maui Resort & Spa offers a tropical escape unlike any other. Watch the spectacular sunset cliff dive ceremony daily, snorkel with sea turtles in our crystal-clear waters, and immerse yourself in Hawaiian culture.

Spanning 23 lush oceanfront acres, our resort features a sprawling lagoon pool, oceanfront dining experiences, and the rejuvenating Spa at Black Rock. Every detail is curated to reflect the spirit of Aloha and ensure a deeply relaxing Maui vacation.
